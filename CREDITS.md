## Art

- **Walk, attack, hurt, sleep and idle animations (camp, Poké-Arena) and dialogue portraits:** [PMD Sprite Collab](https://sprites.pmdcollab.org) - free for fan games with credit to the
  artists, non-commercial only. About half of the sprites (mostly Gen 1-4) are extracted from the Pokémon Mystery
  Dungeon games (credited in the collab to **CHUNSOFT**); the other half (mostly Gen 5 and newer) were made by fan
  artists, who are listed by name under "Walk-sprite artists" below. The dialogue portraits (rival, starter screens, tips) come from the same collab (`portrait/<dex number>/`, credits in the matching `credits.txt`; they are streamed from the collab, not redistributed here). Portrait artists are not yet listed by name in this file: the same collab credit files apply (open item: extend `scripts/generate-credits.mjs` to list them). The collab's own credit data is at
  https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab (`sprite/<dex number>/credits.txt`, `credit_names.txt`).
  Re-run `node scripts/generate-credits.mjs` to refresh the list.
- **Battle sprites, battle backgrounds, move-effect sprites and trainer sprites:** the images served by
  [Pokémon Showdown](https://pokemonshowdown.com): fan-made sprite work by many artists, used non-commercially. The static Gen 5 style Pokémon sprites, trainer sprites and the seven battle backdrops are downloaded into `public/` by `npm run assets` and bundled for offline use (the animated sprites and move effects are still loaded from Showdown). Pokémon is a trademark of Nintendo, Game Freak and The Pokémon Company.

- **Move-effect images, licenses in detail:** the images in Pokémon Showdown's `fx/` folder are CC0 (public domain), except
  `icicle.png` and `lightning.png` by **Clint Bellanger** (triple-licensed GPLv2 / GPLv3 / CC-BY-SA-3.0;
  [Icicle spell](http://opengameart.org/content/icicle-spell), [Lightning shock spell](http://opengameart.org/content/lightning-shock-spell)),
  `bone.png` (same triple license, per the Showdown client's licensing note) and `rocks.png`, `rock1.png`, `rock2.png` by
  "Gilad" (GPLv3). Source and notes: https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown-client (see the licensing note at the top of
  `play.pokemonshowdown.com/src/battle-animations.ts`).
- **Move animations:** the per-move animation recipes (which effect image flies where, when) are adapted from Pokémon
  Showdown's `battle-animations-moves.ts` (CC0-1.0, by Guangcong Luo and contributors).

## Audio

- **Music and sound effects:** original, synthesized in code with the Web Audio API (`src/audio.ts`); no audio files are
  used and no existing Pokémon music is reproduced.
- **Pokémon cries:** streamed from Pokémon Showdown's audio folder (hot-linked, not redistributed here).

## Font

- **Jersey 10** by the Soft Type Project (https://github.com/scfried/soft-type-jersey), licensed under the SIL Open Font
  License 1.1, bundled through `@fontsource/jersey-10`.

## Code and data

- **Battle engine and Pokémon data:** [Pokémon Showdown simulator](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown) via
  `@pkmn/sim` and `@pkmn/dex` (MIT). The battle text ("protected itself!", "But it failed!", ...) comes from
  `@pkmn/view` and `@pkmn/protocol` (MIT).
- **Game framework:** [Phaser](https://phaser.io) (MIT), built with [Vite](https://vite.dev) and TypeScript.

## Item and type icons

The small item icons (balls, potions, stones, held items, ...) and the type icons come from the PokéAPI sprites repository (https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), downloaded by `npm run icons` into `public/icons/`. The original artwork is copyright Nintendo / Game Freak / The Pokémon Company; this is a personal, non-commercial fan project and no rights to the artwork are claimed.

## Camp and arena tilesheets

HGSS for RMXP tilesheets by SirMalo (Eevee Expo, https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/462/), ripped from Pokémon HeartGold/SoulSilver, copyright Nintendo / Game Freak / The Pokémon Company, used non-commercially in a fan game. Stored in `public/tiles/` and used for the camp scene, the Poké-Arena floors and the route demos (`src/tileArt.ts`, `src/campArt.ts`, `src/arenaTiles.ts`).
